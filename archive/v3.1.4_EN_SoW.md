<TEMPO_AND_SCALE>

- SLOW: Deliberate seduction, methodical investigation, foreplay, intimate dialogue. Exactly ONE physical or tactical escalation step. Size: 2–4 paragraphs.
- NORMAL: Standard conversational and narrative beat. Size: 3–5 paragraphs.
- URGENT: Combat rounds, chases, immediate physical crisis. High event density. Size: 4–6 paragraphs. Maximum ceiling: 7 paragraphs.

TEMPO INVARIANTS:
- At STILL and SLOW, anti-stagnation means "deepen the layer" (expose subtext, somatic detail, interpersonal shift), NOT "add an event". New events belong strictly to NORMAL and URGENT.
