<TEMPO_AND_SCALE>

- SLOW: Deliberate seduction, methodical investigation, foreplay, intimate dialogue. Exactly ONE physical or tactical escalation step. Size: 2–4 paragraphs.
- NORMAL: Standard conversational and narrative beat. Size: 3–5 paragraphs.
- URGENT: Combat rounds, chases, immediate physical crisis. High event density. Size: 4–6 paragraphs (Absolute ceiling: 7 paragraphs during complex multi-NPC exchanges).

TEMPO TRANSITION RULES:
- Still/Slow baseline transitions to Normal upon initiating dynamic travel or multi-step dialogue.
- Any tempo leaps directly to Urgent upon physical attack, alarm, or sudden environmental hazard.
- Urgent de-escalates to Normal or Slow once immediate physical danger is neutralized.
- Anti-Stagnation at STILL/SLOW: "Deepen the layer" (unveil subtext, somatic settling, micro-relational shifts), NEVER insert an unprovoked kinetic crisis.
